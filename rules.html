⚖ BLOCKHEAVEN RULEBOOK ⚖

Ignorance Of The Rules Is Not An Excuse

🔗 Account Linking

All players MUST link their Discord account with their Minecraft account for: authentication, events, support, moderation, and security.

🐄 Sacred Cow Protection

Murder of cows is strictly forbidden. Cows are sacred creatures within BlockHeaven. Unnecessary slaughter may result in punishment.

⚔ Combat Rules

No spawn killing, combat logging, unfair targeting, or PvP exploits.

🏠 Griefing & Raiding

Respect builds and community areas. Random destruction and theft are prohibited.

💬 Chat Conduct

No hate speech, harassment, excessive toxicity, or spam.

🛠 Hacks & Exploits

Hacked clients, xray, dupes, macros, and exploits are forbidden.

🎁 Events

Respect event staff and follow all event instructions fairly.
